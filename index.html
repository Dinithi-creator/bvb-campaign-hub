Executive Summary — Build vs Buy is an always-on demand generation campaign targeting asset-intensive organizations evaluating whether to build internal Asset Investment Planning (AIP) capability or invest in an enterprise platform. The campaign reframes the build decision as a strategic risk — surfacing the hidden costs of bespoke tools and positioning IFS Copperleaf as the proven path to scalable, defensible capital decision-making. The campaign runs Feb–Dec 2026 across paid social, programmatic display, content marketing, and lead nurture.

Core messaging framework
Three primary messages and supporting narrative pillars
01
Building internal AIP tools delivers software — buying IFS Copperleaf delivers strategic capability
Internal builds rarely mature into scalable, defensible decision-making platforms. They tend to remain tools rather than strategic enablers, constrained by the skills, time, and priorities of the teams that maintain them.
Supporting angles

Point solutions and custom models often fail when scrutiny, scale, and complexity increase · Enterprise planning is a governance challenge, not just a software challenge

02
The true cost of build is long-term risk, not short-term savings
Homegrown tools increase technical debt, governance risk, and dependency on key individuals. What appears to be a cost-saving strategy often becomes a long-term liability that is difficult to unwind.
Supporting angles

Defensibility matters as much as optimization · Strategic agility depends on fast, credible scenario analysis

03
IFS Copperleaf provides a proven, evidence-led path to better capital outcomes
Delivering measurable ROI, faster time to value, stronger regulatory defensibility, and capital agility at scale — IFS Copperleaf is the enterprise-grade platform that replaces internal builds with strategic decision capability.

Sales enablement & field guidance
How sales teams should use this campaign across discovery, qualification, and objection handling

Guidance for field teams — Use Build vs Buy assets to open conversations with prospects in active evaluation or early dissatisfaction with internal tools. The Readiness Calculator works well as a pre-meeting send or post-call follow-up. The whitepaper is best suited to senior stakeholders — CFO, CIO, Head of Capital Planning — who are sponsoring or evaluating a Build vs Buy decision.

Discovery prompts

  • What tools are you currently using for asset investment planning — spreadsheets, bespoke models, or a dedicated platform?
  • How long has your current approach been in place, and who maintains it internally?
  • Have you experienced challenges presenting your capital plan to regulators or the board?
  • How do you currently handle scenario analysis and trade-off decisions across asset classes?
  • When your key planning team members leave, what happens to the model?
  • What would it mean to have an auditable, defensible capital plan available on demand?

Objection-handling themes

  • "We can build it ourselves." — Internal builds deliver software, not strategic capability. Explore who maintains it in three years.
  • "It's cheaper to build." — Surface TCO: maintenance, technical debt, key-person risk, and the cost of delayed decisions.
  • "We already have a model that works." — Probe: Can it withstand regulatory scrutiny? Does it scale across all asset classes and regions?
  • "We don't have budget." — Reframe around the cost of poor capital decisions relative to the platform investment.
  • "A platform didn't stick last time." — Explore governance gaps; position Copperleaf's time-to-value and support model.
